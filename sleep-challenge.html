Week by Week

What you do
each week.

W1
The Breath Practice
Days 1–7 · Foundation
Every night before sleep — 4 counts in through the nose, 8 counts out through the nose. Five minutes lying down. Lips together. No phone. No screen. Just the breath.

If you wake at 2am — same practice. Three cycles. Then back to sleep.
W2
Add Mouth Tape
Days 8–14 · Lock in nasal breathing
Small piece of surgical tape across the centre of the lips. Start night 1 by wearing it for 20 minutes before you sleep, then remove it. By night 3–4 sleep with it on. By night 7 it's normal.

Morning check — is your mouth moist or dry? Moist means nasal breathing. That's the signal.
W3
Add Morning Tracking
Days 15–21 · Make the change visible
Every morning, score five things before you get out of bed. Write it down or use the tracker below. Thirty seconds. Same time every day. The pattern only appears in the data — not in how you feel on any one morning.
W4
Consolidate and Measure
Days 22–30 · See your result
All three layers running. Breath practice, mouth tape, morning tracking. At day 30 — compare your scores to day 1. That difference is your result. Post it in the WINS section of the community.
